I have just published STALZONE Bot, a Discord bot I built for the STALZONE game community. It recognizes items on inventory screenshots with OpenCV template matching and Tesseract OCR, prices them, and runs boost orders, resource buyouts, reputation and shelter upgrade services through ticket channels. Prices stay up to date automatically: the bot syncs with the in-game auction API and a Google Sheet. The stack is Python, discord.py, OpenCV and gspread, and the whole bot is a single self-contained bot.py. Feel free to take a look: https://github.com/WERTIX182/stalzone-bot
